Our Heritage & Craft

The Art of Bengaluru Vegetarian Cooking

Rooted in Udupi culinary discipline, daily stone-grinding, fresh coastal coconut chutneys, and unadulterated pure dairy ghee on Marathahalli Outer Ring Road.

Daily Morning Ritual

Preparation begins hours before sunrise.

An authentic South Indian kitchen is defined by its morning rhythm. Long before our doors open at 6:30 AM, our cooks begin with the stone-grinding of soaked urad dal and parboiled rice. We adhere strictly to natural room-temperature fermentation: no baking sodas, no chemical conditioners, and no hurried shortcuts.

This patience is what gives our idlis their soft, airy cloud texture and ensures our dosas crisp uniformly with a deep, appetizing golden lattice.

Our chicory-coffee blend is dripped through brass percolation filters in small batches. Combined with unadulterated whole milk, it produces the rich, velvety crema and deep aroma that Bangaloreans expect from their morning cup of degree coffee.

Pure Dairy Ghee

Roasted with genuine dairy ghee. We do not use adulterated cooking fats or artificial aroma compounds.

Strictly Pure Veg

A 100% vegetarian establishment from sourcing to utensils. Completely egg-free and mindful of traditions.

Filtered Water

All sambar, tea, coffee decoction, and drinking water uses multi-stage filtration for consistent taste.

Small-Batch Freshness

Chutneys are ground freshly across all shifts so that customers never receive dry, oxidized accompaniments.

Neighbourhood Hospitality

A welcoming gathering place for Marathahalli.

Located conveniently on Outer Ring Road opposite Kala Mandir, Ashirvad Veg welcomes a cross-section of Bangalore every day: corporate professionals grabbing a quick morning coffee before work, families gathering for weekend dosas, and commuters seeking comforting, hygienic meals.

We provide prompt counter service for those on a tight schedule, as well as a clean, air-cooled dining space for those who prefer to sit and savor a full South Indian thali.
