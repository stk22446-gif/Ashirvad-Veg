Kitchen Discipline & Heritage

Wholesome food cooked with uncompromised integrity.

At Ashirvad Veg, our cooking is rooted in time-tested Udupi vegetarian traditions. Every day begins at 4:00 AM with stone-grinding soaked sona masuri rice and whole urad dal for our breakfast batters, allowing them to rise through natural fermentation without artificial baking soda or chemical shortcuts.

Our Karnataka sambar is slow-simmered in large brass vessels with country drumsticks, shallots, and freshly ground coriander, cumin, and fenugreek. We grind our coconut chutney fresh four times throughout the day so that the chutney served at 7:00 PM is as vibrant and fragrant as the morning batch.

•

Stone-Ground Daily Batters: Naturally fermented overnight without artificial rising agents or baking soda.

•

Fresh Coastal Coconut Chutneys: Grated fresh coconuts and roasted lentils ground four times daily. Never refrigerated.

•

Pure Dairy Desi Ghee: Generously roasted in pure dairy ghee. Strictly zero palm oil, zero vanaspati, and no recycled oils.

•

Brass Filter Peaberry Coffee: Traditional 80:20 plantation peaberry decoction dripped through brass filters and aerated with whole milk.
